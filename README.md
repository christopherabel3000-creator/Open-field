# Open Field

Football you play by ear. Call the play, then run it, throw it, or make the tackle, all through 3D sound.

Play in the browser (headphones recommended): https://christopherabel3000-creator.github.io/Open-field/

- Keyboard: arrows to move through plays, Enter to pick. During a play: arrows to cut or throw, Space to juke or tackle, Up for a burst. S for status, R to repeat, Escape to pause.
- Phone: swipe to move through plays, double tap to pick. During a play: swipe to cut or throw, tap to juke or tackle. Two-finger tap to pause.
